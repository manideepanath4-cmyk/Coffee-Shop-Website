Grown with intention

Good coffee starts long before the roast.

Our coffees come from smallholder farms in Colombia’s Huila region, Ethiopia’s Yirgacheffe highlands, and Guatemala’s Antigua valley. At these high elevations, Arabica coffee cherries ripen slowly in cool mountain air. Many farms grow coffee beneath native shade trees, alongside fruit and other crops that support healthy soil and local wildlife.

When the cherries turn ripe, farmers pick them by hand. In Huila and Antigua, the beans are typically washed and patio-dried for a clean, balanced cup; Yirgacheffe lots may be naturally dried with the fruit left on, bringing out their vivid floral and fruit notes. We roast each origin in small batches to let the character of its home shine through.

From highland farms to your morning cup — sourced with care and roasted fresh.
